# Syllable audio in 120 words

Module 1 build-ups (στώ… ριστώ… ευχαριστώ) cover 86 words and 241 pieces. Every piece is now a word ending, so each needs one cut from the whole-word audio.

**This week:** you voice lessons 1, 10 and 30 locally (the cloud session has no OpenAI key or access) and score ten TTS tricks: hyphens, slow speed, instructed gpt-4o-mini-tts.

**If the tricks fail:**
- **Plan A:** two hours of hand cuts in Audacity, which also become Plan B's answer key.
- **Plan B:** automatic cuts. Signal energy (B1), Greek wav2vec2 forced alignment (B2) and ElevenLabs timestamps (B3) vote; QA flags doubtful cuts.
- **Plan C, Komma:** a February web tool with a waveform and draggable dashed cut lines. Staff fix only flagged cuts.
